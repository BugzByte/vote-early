These open houses are about community, conversation and participation. Come for the food, drinks and company. Ask questions, share information, bring a friend and make your plan to vote.

Already voted? Come anyway! Haven't voted yet? That's great too. Bring your ballot if you'd like, or simply come spend some time with your community.

Make a Plan to Vote

Whether you vote early, vote by mail or vote in person, the important thing is to have a plan. Here are a few places to get started.

Important 2026 General Election Dates

  • October 5 — Voter registration deadline 11:59 PM
  • October 7 — Early voting begins & early ballots are mailed
  • October 23 — Last day to request an early ballot by mail
  • October 27 — Last recommended day to mail your completed ballot
  • November 3 — Election Day
